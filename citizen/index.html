How GramSetu Works

Simple 3-step digital redressal process

1

Register with Mobile

Sign up using your 10-digit mobile number and name. Your account is automatically tied to XYZ Gram Panchayat, Buldhana.

2

Upload Photo & Location

Select category (Roads, Water, Electricity, Sanitation), describe the exact landmark, and attach a photo from your phone or camera.

3

Official Review & Resolution

Panchayat officers examine the issue, update the status from Submitted to In Progress and Resolved, and attach remarks.
